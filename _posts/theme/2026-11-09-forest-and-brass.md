# Forest and Brass

A classic reading theme with dark forest headings, brass accents and a cream background. Tables, quotes and code blocks use the same palette, and text colours meet WCAG AA contrast on the background.

## Install

Download the theme from the [repository](https://github.com/ello-lithuania/typora-steadfast-themes), copy `forest-and-brass.css` to your Typora theme folder (Preferences > Appearance > Open Theme Folder), restart Typora and choose **Forest and Brass** in the Themes menu.
