# Italian N. B

Vintage Italian stationery with a modern layout: ivory paper with a light grain, midnight-blue ink, Pompeian red and old gold.

![Italian N. B theme in Typora](/media/theme/italian-nb/italian-nb.jpg)

## Highlights

- **Ivory paper** with a light grain, resting on a desk-colored background.
- **Classic Italian type:** Optima titles, Bodoni small caps, Palatino for reading and Futura for labels.
- **Front matter as a label** in Futura, between two old-gold rules.
- **Stationery details:** a double rule under the title, a fleuron on horizontal rules, a ledger margin for code blocks and checked tasks drawn as wax seals.
- **Print-friendly:** printing and PDF export drop the desk background.

## Installation

1. Download the theme and unzip it.
2. In Typora, open **Preferences → Appearance → Open Theme Folder**.
3. Copy `italian-n.-b.css` into that folder (Typora needs this exact file name to show and load "Italian N. B").
4. Restart Typora and choose **Themes → Italian N. B**.

## Notes

> Designed and tested on macOS (Typora 1.14). Not fully tested, but should work on Windows and Linux. This theme does not include styles for the Windows "unibody" window style.

Fonts: Optima, Bodoni 72, Palatino and Futura, all included with macOS.
