# MiniChicken for Typora

A light theme that keeps the typography and layout of Typora's built-in Night theme and applies the warm [Gruvbox Light](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox) color palette. The writing surface uses `#FBF1C7`, with brown text and accents, and a highlight color of `#EBD57F`.

![MiniChicken preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thiago-tadeu-goulart/typora-theme-minichicken/main/screenshots/preview.png)

## Install

1. Download and unzip the theme.
2. In Typora, open **Preferences → Appearance → Open Theme Folder**.
3. Copy `minichicken.css` and the `MiniChicken` folder into that folder.
4. Restart Typora and choose **Minichicken** from the **Themes** menu.

Designed and tested on Windows. Not fully tested on macOS or Linux, but it should work there.
