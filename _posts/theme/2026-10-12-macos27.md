# macOS 27 Themes for Typora

Three themes that bring the macOS 27 design language to Typora: SF Pro and SF Mono through the system font stack, rounded surfaces with hairline borders, soft popover shadows and the system blue accent.

- **macOS 27**: soft greys that follow the system appearance, Light by day and Dark at night.
- **macOS 27 Developer**: the crisper grounds of developer.apple.com, pure white in Light and pure black in Dark. Also follows the system appearance.
- **macOS 27 Dark Contrast**: the Dark palette on a deeper near-black background. Always dark.

Every text pair meets at least 4.5:1 contrast and every control and focus indicator at least 3:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA), in every variant and appearance.

## Installation

1. Download the [zipped project](https://github.com/christoarmani/typora-theme-macos27/archive/refs/heads/main.zip) and unzip it.
2. In Typora, open **Settings › Appearance › Open Theme Folder**.
3. Copy the `macos27*.css` files into the theme folder. No fonts or folders needed.
4. Restart Typora and choose **Macos27**, **Macos27 Developer** or **Macos27 Dark Contrast** from the **Themes** menu.

To have the light/dark variants switch with macOS, set the same theme for both Light and Dark in **Settings › Appearance**.

## What's styled

- Headings, body text, links, lists, task lists, quotes and footnotes
- Inline code and fenced code blocks with syntax colours, line numbers and the language picker
- Tables with rounded corners and zebra rows
- GitHub-style callouts: Note, Tip, Important, Warning and Caution
- Math blocks, Mermaid diagrams and YAML front matter
- Outline and Files sidebars, search, Quick Open, menus, tooltips, dialogs and the zoom popup
- Source mode, focus mode and print/PDF export

## Compatibility

Designed and tested on macOS with Typora 1.x. Not fully tested on Windows or Linux: fonts fall back to the platform defaults, and the Windows "unibody" window style is not included.
