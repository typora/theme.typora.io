YOUNG is a pair of quiet light and dark themes for Simplified Chinese and English writing. Soft grayscale colors, serif Chinese text, Courier New Latin text, and a narrow column keep the page simple.

## Typography previews

![Young Light](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZiyanLo/young-typora/main/screenshots/young-light.png)

![Young Dark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZiyanLo/young-typora/main/screenshots/young-dark.png)

## Features

- Matching Young Light and Young Dark variants.
- 15px body text, 1.5 line height, and a 680px writing column.
- Bundled Regular and SemiBold Chinese fonts derived from Adobe Source Han Serif SC, distributed under SIL OFL 1.1.
- Coordinated tables, code, sidebar, selection, and print styles.
- Consistent sidebar fonts and two-line file summaries.

## Installation

Download and unzip the theme. In Typora, open **Preferences → Appearance → Open Theme Folder**. Copy `young-light.css`, `young-dark.css`, and the entire `young-fonts` folder into it. Restart Typora, then select **Young Light** or **Young Dark**.

The font folder is required. Courier New is a system fallback and is not redistributed.

Designed and used on macOS. Browser rendering checked. Windows and Linux have not been tested; no specific Windows unibody styling is included. Native macOS window-title fonts are not controlled by theme CSS.
